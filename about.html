A Bit About Josh

I'm originally from San Diego, CA (about twenty minutes from the Mexico border), and now a senior at Stanford studying Design.

At my core, I love people. Their stories, their energy, and the small moments that make life feel meaningful. Whether it's through my design work, my digital camera photography, or the other ways I spend my free time, I try to amplify the authentic human experience in everything I do.
