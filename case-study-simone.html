Project Overview
During an 8-week internship partnered with Stanford's Global Studies Program, I joined Simone Acc. Collection, a leather goods manufacturer based in Seoul, South Korea, working across both their in-house design team (0914) and their Global Sales team (Tory Burch).

Goal 03
Strengthen Design Visualization
Communicate ideas clearly through concept sketching and design pitch decks, moving efficiently from concept to sample.
Goal 04
Experience Korea
Make the most of my first time working and traveling abroad.
Understanding Simone
What Is Simone?
Simone Acc. Collection is a leading Korean luxury handbag and accessories manufacturer, known for producing high-quality leather goods for global designer brands.
What Is 0914?
0914 is Simone's own premium leather-goods brand, built to showcase the company's craftsmanship, material quality, and design expertise. The brand focuses on timeless, human-centered, small-batch design.
Wing 01
0914 — The Design Team
Working within Simone's in-house design team on individual projects, translating seasonal trends into 0914's next collection.
02Research & Needfinding
Understanding Trends
Trend Research
01 / 12

Using resources from Simone's design team, I researched proposed trends for the FW 26/27 season and built a style guide for the team to reference, thinking through general themes, color palettes, and silhouettes.

Common threads: ornamentalism, functionality, pockets, and playful looks balanced with earth tones.

User Group — 0914
0914 Customers
How might we meld current fashion trends with 0914's brand?
0914 customers value artisan-level craftsmanship and high-quality materials. They struggle to find leather goods on the market that feel uniquely human, rather than designed for the mass market.
03Ideation
Mood Boarding

Pulling from both FW 26/27 trends and elements of 0914's brand identity:

  • Handcrafted design
  • Geologic: earth-tones + metallic elements
  • Intentional design
  • Artistic + vintage styling

This board reflects a direction rooted in tactility, slow craftsmanship, and objects that carry a sense of history.

Mood Board
01 / 06
Materials
01 / 02

There was a plethora of fabrics at our disposal, ranging from cow, sheep, goat, and vegetable leather, as well as suedes and linings. The wide variety let us explore different textures, structures, and finishes while conceptualizing designs.

Sketching
Sketches
01 / 02

Based on the mood boards and materials I'd gathered, I began sketching ideas, leaning into "handcrafted design" through woven materials and canvas that could be custom-dyed to create unique patterns.

Contrast between materials mattered a lot to me here. It plays well into the resurgence of vintage fashion, and it's something I genuinely enjoy as a designer.

04Final Designs — 0914
Final Concepts

I created 3 sketches I felt could realistically be implemented in 0914's next seasonal collection, including Interwoven, a design highlighting hand-crafted construction through woven materials, and Canvas, a silhouette featuring a custom-dyeable canvas panel that gives each piece its own pattern-driven identity.

At my final concept pitch, I presented my designs to 0914's design team: sketches, a digital mockup, mood boards, and leather samples of my proposed materials.

06Needfinding
User Group — Tory Burch
Young Women (20s–30s)
How can I create pieces that feel unique to me as a designer, while also satisfying our target audience?
Tory Burch customers are young, ambitious women stepping into their professional lives, looking for pieces polished yet versatile enough to carry them from the office to evening plans.
Market Research

Visited a luxury department store and several galleries to get a sense of popular trends in the high-end leather accessory industry.

  • Lots of hanging charms
  • Heavy usage of chains
  • High barrier to entry: pricing not very accessible for younger buyers

07Ideation & Prototyping
Sketching + Ideation
01 / 02

Each week, my task was to create one physical sample tailored to the sub-team I was assigned, while still bringing my own point of view as a designer, sketching broadly and experimenting with materials popular for that sub-team.

Materials
01 / 02

As with 0914, there was a wide range of fabrics available: cow, sheep, goat, and vegetable leather, plus suedes and linings. The biggest difference: each Tory Burch sub-team used very specific, distinct materials, which I had to factor into every prototype.

Protos + Patterns
10-Minute Prototypes

One of my supervisors challenged me to grab a few scrap pieces of leather and construct a bag prototype from a sketch, in 10 minutes.

From Sketches to Digital Models

Once sketches were finalized, they were passed to the Patterns team, who built digital models of the designs, ultimately sent to the Sample Room to be physically prototyped by the Stitch Masters.

Sampling
01 / 02

I checked in with the Stitch Masters as they worked from printed versions of the digital model, following up regularly to make sure things were on track, with occasional conversations about hand-feel, hardware choices, and general construction.

09Reflections
Revisiting Goal 01
A Clearer Design POV
Working alongside incredibly creative designers pushed me to trust my own instincts and articulate ideas with more clarity. I also got to attend a fashion show for the first time, a lifelong dream that expanded my creative perspective and shaped the direction of my own design voice.
Revisiting Goal 02
Cultural & Market Understanding
Conducting market research in Seoul, one of the trendiest fashion hubs in the world, let me observe emerging street styles and consumer behavior firsthand, strengthening my ability to spot broader global design trends.
Revisiting Goal 03
Design Visualization Skills
I learned that sketching isn't scary. It's just another way to convey ideas, however rough. This was the most I've ever sketched in my life, and I'm grateful I pushed myself further into that side of design.
Revisiting Goal 04
Experience Korea!
Truly one of the most incredible experiences of my life. I explored Seoul, Anyang, Suwon, Sokcho, and Goryeong. Two months full of incredible people, food, and memories made.
